What changed after assembly

On the bench, slight movement in the motor mount caused the gears to lose mesh, and the cup rubbed the frame on the gear side. I revised the mount and checked how the cup and frame fitted through the arm’s travel. Building the prototype showed where assembly and clearance needed more attention than the CAD alone suggested.
