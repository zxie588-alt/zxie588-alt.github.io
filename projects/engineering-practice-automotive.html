Huazhong Agricultural University (undergraduate) · Lab, robotics and automotive training

Engineering Practice & Automotive Structure Training

Undergraduate practical training at Huazhong Agricultural University in three areas: mechanics testing with force-displacement results, desktop robotic-arm control, and an automotive structure training visit.

My role: Trainee. I practised mechanics testing and robotic-arm control, and on the automotive visit I helped explain vehicle interior and structural systems to a small group of fellow students.

TestingForce-displacement results from mechanics testing equipment
RoboticsDesktop robotic-arm control with actuator and controller hardware
AutomotiveSeat, frame, trim, wiring and steering-area structure
ExplainingHelped explain vehicle systems to a small student group

What I took from it

  • Comfort around physical test equipment, not only CAD and simulation tools.
  • Linking a test setup, the measured response and the engineering interpretation.
  • Hands-on mechatronics: robotic-arm control and basic hardware interaction.
  • Automotive structure awareness: seats, trim, wiring, steering area, interior support structures and assembly logic.
  • Explaining technical features face to face to a small group, not only in written reports.

How it supports graduate work

It helps me read test data, ask useful questions about fixtures and mechanisms, communicate clearly in a team, and remember that products are assembled, tested, serviced and explained by real people.
